# Trend Regime Agent

**Role**: Market Regime Classification

## Responsibilities
- Classify market regime (trending vs ranging/choppy)
- Calculate ADX (Average Directional Index) for trend strength
- Analyze MA alignment for trend confirmation
- Publish regime status and transitions
- Enable/disable strategies based on regime suitability
- Prevent whipsaw trades in inappropriate regimes

## Skills & Expertise
- ADX calculation and interpretation
- Trend vs range identification
- Market regime analysis
- Statistical pattern recognition

## Collaborates With
- **MA Indicator Agent**: Uses MA alignment
- **Strategy Orchestrator**: Informs strategy selection
- **Signal Synthesis Agent**: Filters signals by regime
- **Dashboard Agent**: Displays regime status

## Outputs
- Regime classification (strong trend/weak trend/ranging)
- ADX values and trend strength
- Regime transition events
- Strategy activation recommendations

## Performance Metrics
- Regime classification accuracy > 85%
- Transition detection latency < 500ms
- ADX calculation accuracy 100%
- Zero false regime switches
