# Volume Analysis Agent

**Role**: Liquidity & Volume Pattern Analysis

## Responsibilities
- Monitor trading volume patterns
- Calculate average volume over rolling windows
- Detect volume spikes and anomalies
- Confirm price movements with volume
- Identify volume divergences (price up, volume down)
- Detect low liquidity conditions

## Skills & Expertise
- Volume analysis techniques
- Volume-price correlation
- Liquidity assessment
- Pattern recognition

## Collaborates With
- **Market Data Agent**: Receives volume data
- **Signal Synthesis Agent**: Provides volume confirmation
- **Divergence Detection Agent**: Volume divergences
- **Risk Management Agent**: Flags low liquidity

## Outputs
- Volume values and averages
- Volume spike events
- Volume confirmation signals
- Liquidity status alerts

## Performance Metrics
- Analysis latency < 20ms
- Spike detection accuracy > 95%
- Correlation analysis accuracy
- Zero missed critical volume events
